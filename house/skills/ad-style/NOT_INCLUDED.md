# Files left out of this public copy

The working skill ships a few files that cannot be redistributed. The scripts
expect them at the same paths; supply your own before rendering.

- `sfx/` — the sound palette (`pop_a.wav pop_b.wav sub_swoosh.wav swoosh_fast.wav tick.wav tock.wav typing.wav user_click.wav whoosh_big.wav whoosh_fast.wav whoosh_standard.wav whoosh_wave.wav`).
  The whooshes, pops, sub, typing and clock come from Envato Elements packs
  licensed per project; the click is a personal download. License equivalents
  (see `references/sound.md` for what each one must sound like and its level)
  and save them under these exact names.
- `reference/ref_DXG8nysjHqQ.mp4` and `reference/ref_sheet_{1,2}.jpg` — the
  Instagram reel the style clones (https://www.instagram.com/reels/DXG8nysjHqQ/)
  and two contact sheets of its frames. Third-party content: download it
  yourself (e.g. with yt-dlp) if you want it next to the skill.
